\section{Related Works}%
\label{sec:related_works}

To the best of our knowledge, little research has been conducted on the formalizations of 
declarative mapping languages for knowledge graph construction.
According to Van Assche et al.~\cite{VanAssche2022DeclarativeRDFgraph}, 
declarative mapping languages can be further categorized into two categories: 
i) \emph{dedicated languages} which extend specifications or use custom
syntaxes, and ii) \emph{repurposed languages} which repurpose existing
specifications or query languages to support knowledge graph construction.
In this section, we briefly review existing works on formalizations of 
dedicated mapping languages for knowledge graph constructions. 

We do not consider repurposed mapping languages in this work since they 
inherit the formalizations of the specifications or query languages on which
they are based upon, limiting them to the syntax of the parent languages and not 
applicable for mapping languages in general.
