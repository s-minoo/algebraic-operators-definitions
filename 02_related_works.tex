When considering dedicated mapping languages, to the best of our knowledge, only R2RML has a formally defined semantics. That is, Sequeda et al.~\cite{Sequeda2013R2RML} provide such a formalization by means of 57 Datalog rules. As this definition is for R2RML, it is limited to mappings from relational databases to RDF, whereas our formalization in this paper can capture mappings of different types of sources of structured and semi-structured data. Moreover, we argue that an algebraic formalization such as ours has the additional advantage that it can also be utilized to represent a form of logical execution plans in mapping engines.

Besides R2RML, a few studies with formalizations related to RML have been published in recent years~\cite{Iglesias2022ScalingKnowledgeGraph,MinOo2023TowardsAlgebra}.
