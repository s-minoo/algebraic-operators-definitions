\section{Related Work}%
\label{sec:related_works}

According to Van Assche et al.~\cite{VanAssche2022DeclarativeRDFgraph},
declarative mapping languages can be categorized into two categories: i)
\emph{dedicated languages} which extend mapping specifications (e.g.
R2RML~\cite{Seema2012R2RML} specifications) or use custom syntaxes, and ii)
\emph{repurposed languages} which repurpose existing non-mapping specifications
or query languages to support KG construction.
In this section, we briefly review existing works on formalizations of 
declarative mapping languages for KG construction.

\subsection{Formalizations in Repurposed Mapping Languages}%
\label{sub:formalizations_in_repurposed_mapping_languages}

To the best of our knowledge, only SPARQL-based mapping languages have defined
formal
semantics~\cite{lefrancois_eswc_2017,Lopes2011XSPARQL,Bischof:2012:XSPARQL,asprino2023knowledge}
in the category of repurposed mapping languages.
SPARQL-Anything~\cite{asprino2023knowledge} utilizes a two-step approach. 
First, it formalizes the Façade-X
meta-model~\cite{Daga2021FacadeXOpinionated}, which transforms heterogeneous
data into an intermediate RDF graph describing the data's structure.
The final step uses SPARQL queries on the Façade-X RDF graph to generate RDF KG
with SPARQL \ttl{CONSTRUCT} query, which is fully compatible with the formal
semantics of SPARQL. 
SPARQL-Generate~\cite{lefrancois_eswc_2017} extends concepts from SPARQL 1.1 to
define the language, including modifications that support KG generation from
heterogeneous data.
XSPARQL~\cite{Bischof:2012:XSPARQL} combines the semantics of XQuery and SPARQL
for mapping between XML and RDF data.
The authors also extend their work to support mapping relational databases to
RDF data by utilizing the semantics of relational
algebra~\cite{Lopes2011XSPARQL}. 

The aforementioned works rely on the semantics of the underlying non-mapping
languages they build upon. 
We argue that reliance on the semantics of the underlying non-mapping language
limits the applicability of the formalism proposed in these works to
dedicated mapping languages specifically designed for mapping heterogeneous
data to generate KGs. 
    
\subsection{Formalizations in Dedicated Mapping Languages}
\label{sub:dedicated_mapping_languages}
R2RML~\cite{Seema2012R2RML} is a dedicated mapping language for R2RML mappings,
which are RDF graphs for mapping relational databases to RDF KG.
These mappings are usually presented using Turtle syntax.
R2RML has been used as the basis for several mapping languages which extend its
scope from relational databases to structured or semi-structured
data~\cite{VanAssche2022DeclarativeRDFgraph}, such as
RML~\cite{dimou_ldow_2014}, D2RML~\cite{Chortaras2018D2RML}, and
xR2RML~\cite{michel2015translation}. 
Thus, it is important to consider the formalization studies conducted on 
R2RML which can have impact on the languages in the R2RML family.  

Sequeda et al.~\cite{Sequeda2013R2RML} provide formalization to the semantics
of R2RML using Datalog.
The authors prove that the semantics of R2RML can be captured using 57 distinct
Datalog rules to generate RDF from relational databases. 
While this formalization is focused on a single type of input data, namely a 
relational database, our approach is designed to capture arbitrary types of 
heterogeneous data sources for mapping. 
Furthermore, Datalog-based formalization limits the accessibility for
developers aiming to implement mapping engines that comply with these
formalisms. 
In contrast, our approach defines algebraic operators in a clear, and concise
manner, making it easier to adopt and implement a working mapping engine.
