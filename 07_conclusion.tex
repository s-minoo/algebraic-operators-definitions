\section{Conclusion}%
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we provide a set of formal definitions using algebraic 
mapping operators to introduce semantics to the process of KG generation 
from heterogeneous data.
The proposed formalism is language-agnostic, and provides a unified model 
for describing mappings used to map heterogeneous data to KG. 
This formalism provides the basis for implementing a mapping engine that 
is capable of supporting multiple declarative mapping languages without 
changes to the implementation.
Additionally, we show that our formalism fully captures the semantics of RML by
providing a translation algorithm that translate the mappings from any RML
document to our formalism. 
Consequently, the formalism is shown to be at least as expressive as RML for
mapping heterogeneous data to KG. 
Lastly, we provide a set of algebraic equivalences to show that mapping plans
generated as a result of our formalism are optimizable.
As future works, this theoretical foundation opens the door to three 
frontiers in the domain of KG generation from heterogeneous data; 
i) a unified algebraic mapping engine, 
ii) further studies into the optimizations of mapping plans based on this formalism, and
iii) expansion upon this formalism to capture the semantics of all existing 
mapping languages.
