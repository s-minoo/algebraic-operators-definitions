\section{Concluding Remarks and Future Work}%
\label{sec:conclude_remark_fw}

In this paper, we provide a set of language-agnostic algebra for 
formalizing the semantics of mapping heterogeneous types of data sources to KG.
%This formalism provides the basis for implementing a mapping engine that is capable of supporting multiple declarative mapping languages without changes to the implementation.
Additionally, we show that our formalism is at least as expressive as RML 
for mapping heterogeneous data to KG, and as a consequence, we formalize the semantics of RML.
Lastly, we provide a set of algebraic rewriting rules to show that mapping plans
generated, as a result of our formalism, are optimizable.
As future work, this theoretical foundation opens the door to three 
frontiers in the domain of KG generation from heterogeneous types of data sources;
i) provably correct implementations of mapping engines, 
ii) further studies into the optimizations of mapping plans based on this formalism, and
iii) expansion upon this formalism to capture the semantics of all existing 
mapping languages.
